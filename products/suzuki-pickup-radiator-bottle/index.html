Suzuki Pickup Radiator Bottle (Coolant Reservoir)

A replacement radiator (coolant) reservoir bottle made for the Suzuki Pickup (Ravi), manufactured in-house at our Lahore facility.

This reservoir bottle holds overflow coolant for the Suzuki Pickup's cooling system, molded in durable plastic to withstand engine-bay heat and vibration.

Vehicle application: Suzuki Pickup / Ravi.

Identification: Bring your existing bottle or a clear photo so we can confirm the correct shape and fitment before you order.
