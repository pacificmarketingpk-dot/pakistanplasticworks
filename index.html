About Pakistan Plastic Works

Pakistan Plastic Works manufactures injection-molded plastic components for the automotive aftermarket, focused on parts for the Suzuki Bolan. Every part is molded in-house to OEM fitment, so mechanics and parts dealers get a consistent, reliable product batch after batch.

We work with individual mechanics, auto parts shops and bulk distributors across Pakistan.
