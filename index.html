Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Pakistan Plastic Works?

Pakistan Plastic Works is a Lahore-based manufacturer of aftermarket plastic replacement parts for Suzuki vehicles, established in early 2010. The company also accepts outsourced plastic manufacturing and labour-based production work for other businesses.

What does Pakistan Plastic Works manufacture?

Pakistan Plastic Works manufactures Suzuki aftermarket plastic parts — including radiator bottles, wiper bottles, plastic center bushes, bearing components, steel pipe bush parts and air pipes — for the Mehran, Cultus, Pickup and Van. It also performs outsourced blow molding, small injection molding, hand injection molding and pipe cutting for other businesses.

Is Pakistan Plastic Works an authorized Suzuki dealer?

No. Pakistan Plastic Works manufactures aftermarket replacement parts for Suzuki vehicles and is not an authorized Suzuki dealer and not officially affiliated with Suzuki Motor Corporation.

Where is Pakistan Plastic Works located?

The factory is located at 87/E Grain Market, Near General Bus Stand, Badami Bagh, Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan.

Does Pakistan Plastic Works sell to individual buyers or only wholesale?

Pakistan Plastic Works serves individual buyers, shops and distributors. Message us on WhatsApp with the part you need and the quantity, and we'll confirm pricing and availability.

Can Pakistan Plastic Works manufacture parts outside its own Suzuki product range?

Yes. Beyond its own Suzuki aftermarket parts, Pakistan Plastic Works accepts outsourced manufacturing projects — see our Manufacturing Services and Outsourcing pages for details.
